🍎 What is WooaMac?

WooaMac is a service that curates official download links for essential apps you need to install after buying a new Mac or resetting macOS, organized by category.

From browsers, security, archivers, media, office, messengers, developer tools, productivity, utilities, to cloud storage — find and install every app your Mac needs, all in one place.

✅ Service Features

  • All links go exclusively to each app's official website or the App Store
  • Curated selection of apps optimized for macOS and Mac-exclusive apps
  • Focused on apps with free core functionality
  • Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) compatibility considered
  • Accessible from any device — PC, tablet, or smartphone
